- Icons + bars/numbers for key metrics: 
 
  - ✅ Customer Satisfaction Meter

  - ⏱ Efficiency/Stress Gauge
 

 
 

 
**Right Side (Coworker Status Board)**
 
 
- List of coworkers (abstract icons by dept).

- Manager support icon appears if 2 unanswered call-outs occur.
 

 
**Bottom Left (Notes Pedestal)**
 
 
- Digital clipboard / notepad view.
